THE PROBLEM

Why do high performers put on the mask?

Pressure. Performance. Expectations.

Over time, they teach people to hide what they are carrying, avoid asking for help, and tie who they are to what they produce. It shows up in trust, communication, leadership, and eventually, performance.

This is not about performing less. It is about performing without losing yourself.
