About Fernando

Fernando played nine years in the NFL, then served as the league's Director of Player Engagement, working with athletes through the pressures of the game and the transitions beyond it.

He is also open about his bipolar I diagnosis. His vulnerability is the foundation of his message: that strength and honesty belong together, and that no one has to carry it alone.

Today he is a speaker, educator, and founder who helps leaders, athletes, and students lead and live without hiding.
