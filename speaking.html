THE M.A.S.K.

High Performance Without Hiding

High performers are taught to push through, produce, and deliver. They are not always taught what to do with everything they carry underneath. Fernando combines nine years in the NFL, years of player development, his own mental health journey, and the M.A.S.K. framework to help audiences recognize what high performance can hide and build a healthier way forward.

This is not about lowering expectations. It is about helping people carry them.
